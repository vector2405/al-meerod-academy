WELCOME TO OUR SCHOOL

Every child deserves the chance to flourish.

Established in 2026, Al-Meerod Academy is located at Old Barracks, beside Touch Hotel, Ilobi Oke, Ajilete, Yewa, Ogun State, Nigeria. We are committed to providing quality education that supports academic excellence, moral discipline and the all-round development of every learner.

Our educational programmes combine Western education with Islamic learning, including Islamic Studies, Tahfeez (Qur'an memorisation) and Arabic Madrasah. We value dedicated teaching, a supportive learning environment, good character and partnership with parents and guardians.
